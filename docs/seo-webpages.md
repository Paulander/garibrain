## Relationship Reminder App

Meta title: Relationship reminder app for important dates and details

Meta description: Remember anniversaries, birthdays, preferences, gift ideas, and follow-ups with a private relationship reminder app.

H1: A relationship reminder app for the details that slip

Body:

Some relationship details are too small for a calendar invite but too important to forget. PartnerOps gives you one private place for dates, preferences, dislikes, gift hints, support patterns, and lessons from past conversations.

Use it to capture:

- Birthdays, anniversaries, first-met dates, and custom dates.
- Lead reminders before a date becomes a scramble.
- Preferences, dislikes, allergies, sizes, and sensitive notes.
- Follow-ups after hard conversations, date nights, trips, or family events.

PartnerOps is built for private planning, not partner surveillance. It does not read messages, import contacts, track location, or disguise itself as another app.

## Private Relationship Notes App

Meta title: Private relationship notes app with local-first storage

Meta description: Store relationship notes privately on your device with app lock, neutral notifications, local export, and delete-all-data controls.

H1: Private notes for showing up better

Body:

PartnerOps is a local-first relationship notes app. V1 has no custom backend, no partner account, no social scraping, no contacts import, no HealthKit or Health Connect, and no location tracking.

Privacy tools include:

- App lock.
- Quick lock.
- Neutral, minimal, or silent notifications.
- Hidden notification previews setting.
- Local export.
- Delete all local data.
- Sensitive-field handling where practical.

Use PartnerOps as a private memory tool, not as a complaint log or surveillance file.

## Cycle Support Reminders

Meta title: Manual cycle support reminders for care planning

Meta description: Save optional manual cycle estimates and recurring support cues for practical care reminders. Not medical advice.

H1: Manual cycle support reminders for practical care

Body:

PartnerOps can store optional manual cycle-based support windows. These are estimates for care planning only. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, or mood prediction.

You can note support cues like:

- Low energy.
- Skin flare-ups.
- Migraine patterns.
- Sleep disruption.
- Wants quiet evenings.
- Practical help that actually works.
- What backfires.

The goal is simple: prepare useful care and better timing. PartnerOps must not be used to blame mood, predict sex, diagnose health, or win arguments.
