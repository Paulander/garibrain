# PartnerOps Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 2026-05-13

PartnerOps is a private relationship memory and planning tool. It is designed to work locally on your device without a custom backend in V1.

## Data You Add

PartnerOps may store information you enter, including partner profile details, important dates, preferences, gift ideas, support patterns, cycle-based support estimates, reminders, and debrief notes.

This information is stored locally on your device. V1 does not create PartnerOps accounts, does not sync to a PartnerOps server, and does not sell or share your data.

## Sensitive Data

Some notes may be sensitive. PartnerOps supports app lock, neutral notifications, hidden previews, quick lock, local export, delete-all-data, and encrypted sensitive fields where practical.

Cycle-based support reminders are manually configured estimates for support planning only. They are not medical advice and may be inaccurate.

## Permissions

PartnerOps requests only permissions needed for V1 functionality:

- Notifications, if you enable local reminders.
- Biometric/local authentication, if you enable app lock.

PartnerOps does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location, HealthKit, Health Connect, calendar read/write access, or social/media scraping permissions.

## Notifications

Notifications are local scheduled reminders. PartnerOps supports privacy modes:

- Full: reminder details may appear.
- Neutral: generic PartnerOps reminder text.
- Minimal: "Reminder."
- Silent: no notification.

Neutral is the recommended default.

## Data Export and Deletion

You can export local app data from Settings. You can delete all local data from Settings. Deleting local data clears the PartnerOps local database on that device.

## AI and Cloud Services

AI is disabled in V1. PartnerOps does not send your notes to an AI service. If AI is added later, it must use a server-side endpoint and explain what context is sent.

## Children

PartnerOps is not intended for children.

## Contact

Support URL and contact email must be added before store submission.
