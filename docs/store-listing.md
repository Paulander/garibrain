## App Store Description

PartnerOps is a private relationship memory and planning app for people who mean well but forget details.

Use it to remember important dates, preferences, dislikes, gift ideas, recurring support patterns, and follow-ups from previous conversations. The Today dashboard turns saved details into practical, calendar-bound prompts so you know what deserves attention now, this week, or before an important date.

PartnerOps is local-first in V1. There are no partner invites, no shared accounts, no backend sync, no location tracking, and no contact or message scraping. Privacy tools include app lock, quick lock, neutral notifications, hidden previews, local export, delete-all-data, and encrypted sensitive fields where practical.

Cycle-based support windows are optional, manual estimates for support planning only. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, or mood prediction.

PartnerOps is not therapy, medical advice, legal advice, or a health diagnosis tool.

## Google Play Full Description

PartnerOps is a private relationship memory and planning app built to help you remember what matters.

Track important dates, preferences, dislikes, gift ideas, support patterns, recurring reminders, and debrief notes from previous conversations or events. PartnerOps turns that saved context into practical prompts for today, this week, and upcoming occasions.

Features:

- Private partner profile
- Important dates and lead reminders
- Preference memory and quick capture
- Optional manually configured support windows
- Optional cycle-based support estimates for care planning
- Rule-based panic helper for gifts, dates, messages, trips, and repair gestures
- Debrief notes for what worked, what backfired, and what to remember next time
- Local notifications with privacy modes
- App lock, quick lock, local export, and delete-all-data

PartnerOps is local-first in V1. It does not use a custom backend, partner accounts, location tracking, contacts, camera, microphone, HealthKit, Health Connect, calendar permissions, or message/social scraping.

Cycle-based reminders are estimates for support planning only. They are not medical advice and may be inaccurate.

PartnerOps is not therapy, medical advice, legal advice, or a health diagnosis tool.
